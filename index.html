How it works

Four simple steps from booking to transport

The process stays clear and practical, which matters when families are arranging transport under pressure.

Step 01

Call or WhatsApp us

Send the pickup point, destination and timing. If the situation is urgent, a direct call is the fastest way to confirm availability.

Step 02

Share the requirement

Tell us whether you need an ambulance, patient transport, mortuary vehicle, ice box or long-distance move so we can match the right vehicle.

Step 03

Confirm the vehicle

We check what is available at that time and confirm the route, service type and any equipment needed for the transport.

Step 04

Transport is arranged

Once confirmed, the vehicle is dispatched and the trip is coordinated carefully until the patient reaches the destination.
